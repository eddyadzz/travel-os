## 4 · Deployment process

1. **Provision** — dedicated server + database for your agency.
2. **Configure** — your domain, email provider, branding and users.
3. **Import** — your properties, rooms, rates, suppliers, customers and leads
   (using provided Excel templates with validation).
4. **Verify** — automated readiness check (backup, email, website, data).
5. **Train** — administrators, agents and your suppliers (portals).
6. **Go live** — we confirm the public site, customer portal and automation are active.
