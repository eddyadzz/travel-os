# Client Owner Guide

This guide is for the agency owner / management team who wants to understand revenue,
profitability, forecasting and team performance — without touching day-to-day
operations.

## Command center (`/command`)

The daily overview that answers the questions owners actually ask:

- **Expected revenue this month**, confirmed revenue and expected profit
- **Needs attention**: leads to follow up, open quotes, overdue deposits, supplier
  requests, suppliers at risk, contracts renewing
- **Arrivals next 14 days**
- **Contracts renewing within 90 days**
- **Agent performance** this year

## Finance (`/finance`)

- **Profit & Loss** — per booking: revenue, supplier cost, gross profit, margin %,
  plus totals. This is your true agency margin.
- **Outstanding** — every booking with an unpaid balance.
- **Deposits** and **Payment ledger** — money in, by type and status.
- **Agent performance** — bookings, revenue, profit and commission per agent.
- **Exports** — CSV, Excel, QuickBooks and Xero journals for your accountant.
- **Accounting sync** — one-click generated Xero/QuickBooks journals stored for the
  period, downloadable anytime.

## Forecasting (`/intelligence`)

- **Revenue forecast** (next 90 days): confirmed + pipeline → expected collections and
  expected profit at your actual margin.
- **Booking forecast**: open quotes × conversion → expected confirmations;
  expected cancellations from history.
- **Lead intelligence**: which channels convert (by source), average booking value,
  best-performing source.
- **Supplier risk**: response times trending worse, contracts expiring.

## Supplier scorecards (`/scorecards`)

Suppliers are ranked automatically: confirmation rate, average response time,
cancellation rate, booking volume, revenue, profit and contract expiry — with a
composite score and a **Reliable / Needs attention / At risk** tier.

## AI assistant (`/assistant`)

- **Management** — ask in plain English: "expected revenue this month?",
  "which deposits are overdue?", "which suppliers are at risk?", "which leads are
  likely to convert?"
- **Sales** — recommend properties for an enquiry (budget, guests, preferences),
  with markup applied and a draft quote message.
- **Operations** — auto-drafted follow-ups for slow suppliers, expiring contracts
  and overdue deposits.

## Key numbers to watch

| Metric | Where |
|--------|-------|
| Gross margin % | Finance → P&L |
| Expected revenue / profit | Intelligence → Revenue forecast |
| Outstanding balances | Finance → Outstanding |
| Overdue deposits | Command center |
| Lead conversion by source | Intelligence → Lead intelligence |
| Supplier reliability | Scorecards |
| Agent profit + commission | Finance → Agent performance |
| Contracts expiring | Command center / Scorecards |
